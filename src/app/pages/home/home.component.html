Who we are

Making a Difference, One Life at a Time

Established in August 2025, Jeevan Foundation Trust is a public charitable trust based in Karadiputhur, Uthukkottai Taluk, Tiruvallur District, Tamil Nadu. Our initiatives focus on women empowerment, healthcare outreach, child welfare, disability inclusion, rural livelihoods, senior citizen care, and legal rights awareness.

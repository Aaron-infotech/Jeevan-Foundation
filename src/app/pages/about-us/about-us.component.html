About Jeevan Foundation Trust

Jeevan Foundation Trust is a public charitable trust established in August 2025 and based in Karadiputhur, Uthukkottai Taluk, Tiruvallur District, Tamil Nadu.

The trust works at the grassroots level to support humanitarian relief, healthcare outreach, education, women empowerment, disability inclusion, rural livelihoods, senior citizen welfare, and legal rights awareness.

We believe in equal opportunity, inclusive dignity, and community-led progress regardless of caste, religion, gender, economic status, or background.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive and compassionate society where every person has access to opportunities, essential support, and the resources needed to live with dignity.

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities through social welfare initiatives, education, healthcare awareness, inclusive development, and sustainable livelihood opportunities.
